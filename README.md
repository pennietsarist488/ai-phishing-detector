- Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge browser.
- A stable internet connection.
- At least 4GB of RAM.

## ⚙️ Installation Steps

Follow these steps to link the tool to your browser:

1. Open Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
2. Type `chrome://extensions` or `edge://extensions` in the address bar and press Enter.
3. Toggle the switch in the bottom-left or top-right corner to turn on "Developer mode".
4. Click the button labeled "Load unpacked".
5. Find the folder where you extracted the tool files. 
6. Click "Select Folder".
7. The browser will add the extension to your toolbar. You will see the icon appear near your address bar.

## 🚀 Running the System

The system operates quietly in the background. After you install the extension, it starts every time you open your browser.

1. Browse the web as you normally do.
2. Click the extension icon if you want to see the status of your security.
3. If the tool detects a site that looks like a scam, a window will pop up to warn you.
4. Do not enter personal details on any page flagged by the red warning indicator.

## 🛠️ Frequently Asked Questions

**Does this tool slow down my computer?**
The tool uses efficient processing methods to ensure your browser remains fast. Most users do not notice a change in performance.

**Why does it need the Flask backend?**
The software splits the workload. Your browser handles the visual parts, while the background service manages the complex data analysis required for security.

**What should I do if the extension stops working?**
Open your extension page and click the refresh icon on the phishing detector card. If that does not help, remove the folder and reload it using the installation steps.

**Can I trust the software?**
The software runs locally on your machine. Your browsing data does not leave your computer. We prioritize your privacy alongside your security.

## 🛡️ Privacy Policy

This tool processes URLs locally. It does not send your passwords or credit card numbers to any servers. The security engine checks the reputation of a website based on its structure and known traits of phishing pages.
